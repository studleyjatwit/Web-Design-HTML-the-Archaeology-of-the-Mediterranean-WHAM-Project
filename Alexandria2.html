Alexandria served as one of the most important cities in both the Roman Empire and the Egyptian world. Select a period on the timeline below to see how the city's geography and landmarks changed over time.

Layer 1: Alexander the Great & The Master Plan (332–323 BCE)

Alexander chooses the coastal strip between Lake Mareotis and the Mediterranean beside the native village of Rhakotis. Architect Dinocrates lays out the orthogonal grid along the Canopic Way, and Ptolemy I later entombs Alexander's body at the Soma.
